04 / A thoughtful exchange

Earned.
Useful.
Limited.

The planned trading approach starts with what a companion genuinely earns—and what it can afford to let go.

Careful limits across the whole companion population.
I.

Earn it through the journey.

Items come from supported activities. The intent is for progress and supplies to have a real source in the game world.

II.

Keep what the character needs.

Training supplies and useful equipment come first. Only genuinely surplus duplicates become candidates for sale.

III.

Offer a little, when it makes sense.

Demand checks and global selling caps are part of the planned rules. Extra stock does not mean unlimited listings.

05 / A few good questions

Know who’s
on the path.

A clear look at what this feature is intended to bring to the world.

Are these human players?

No. These are explicitly server-controlled companions. They are intended to add activity and familiar faces to the world, while the community remains made up of real people.

How will they level up?

The plan is to use supported in-game activities and their normal XP rewards. A main or hybrid can work toward all supported skills; a skiller works toward the noncombat skills its build allows.

Do they disappear when they take a break?

The planned behavior is to pause safely while remaining in the world. Brief pauses and longer rests interrupt the grind without requiring an ordinary logout. Server maintenance and restarts still apply.

What makes a skiller different?

A skiller keeps a persistent noncombat identity. Its activity choices are planned to respect combat XP restrictions, including extra XP that an activity might award to another skill.

Will they overwhelm the market?

The planned approach only considers earned surplus duplicates, protects supplies needed for training, and checks demand and population-wide limits before selling. The goal is a measured presence, with limits that can be tuned after testing.

Is this feature already available?

This page presents a feature in development. Server integration and testing are still required. The characters, build selector, and village scenes are illustrations of the intended experience, not live server data.
