HOLLOW DEPTHS — KRONOS REVISION 184

Getting in
Use the red Kronos Teleporter at home, choose Minigames, select Hollow Depths,
then choose Novice, Veteran, or Nightmare. Be near the home portal, out of
combat, and pick up your pet before entering. Bring your normal combat gear,
food, and potions.

The dungeon
Each run belongs to one account and has a newly generated layout. Explore
14–16 rooms, open treasure chests, and defeat three sigil guardians to unlock
the final boss. Defeat that boss, open its chest, then use the dungeon exit
to secure your haul. The ::hollow command shows progress or lets you abandon.

Traps and doors
Agility shortcuts have different level requirements and can fail. A longer
corridor with enemies is available when you cannot use a shortcut. Click the
door at either end to walk through; it closes behind you. The corridor's mobs
stay inside their area. Both routes reach the same obstacle-room chest.

Rewards and death
Chest treasure is unbanked until you successfully extract. After extraction,
choose Collect secured rewards from the Hollow Depths menu. Resources that
do not fit remain in your mailbox. Dungeon shards and progression fragments
remain account-bound credits; no custom cosmetic redemption shop is included.
Death or abandoning keeps your carried gear and inventory, but loses that
run's unbanked treasure. Completed Agility XP awards are retained.

Disconnects and maintenance
An ordinary disconnect allows up to three minutes to resume the same run
while that server process remains running. Runs expire after 36 minutes,
or nine minutes without activity. Maintenance closes active runs safely and
loses their unbanked treasure; already committed rewards remain secured.
An extraction already being saved is retried using the same transaction.

Compatibility
This implementation uses the existing revision 184 client, cache, and server
protocol. It does not install the separate revision 242 upgrade or require
a new Android APK. The intended 15–20 minute completion time is a design
target; automated fixtures do not establish normal-player completion time.
