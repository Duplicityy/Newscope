All code in this batch is isolated. Feature switches require the schemaVersion2
content242.json registry and explicit source-to-target item/NPC/animation/graphic/
object/region bindings. No missing identifier falls back to a donor ID.

Gear batch: Fang passive/special, Voidwaker special, Inquisitor crush bonuses,
Tumeken's shadow charged spell, Bow of Faerdhinen shots/charges, crystal armour,
Soulreaper stacks/special/decay, Virtus Ancient magic bonus, canonical ancient
sceptre effects. Burning claws and Scorching bow are implemented by the sibling
burn module. Detached gear fixture checks exercise actual native Hit.defend and
item listeners. They are not a completed client/gameplay review.

Private encounters: Scurrius, Amoxliatl and Phantom Muspah. Entry uses ordinary
native teleport permission. Owners, map leases, delayed impacts, actor cleanup
and return-on-login markers are explicit. These initial private instances have
safe deaths and one kill per entry. The normal native death and drop pipeline
handles boss kills. Full quest access, gravestone rules, automatic boss respawn,
combat achievements, collection/scoreboard interfaces and all secondary rewards
are not claimed as ported by these classes.

Muspah: ranged/melee forms; captured attack/special timings; first-form damage
threshold then four-hit switches; darkness specials with homing spikes and20
teleport/cloud jumps; spike knockback and75percent heal; shockwave ray shelter;
75-point prayer shield, native Smite,25percent sapphire-bolt proc,2points drained
per own attack, Soul Split; shield skip/failure; final spreading spikes;
corruption prayer drain;66percent freeze duration; instance-owned125Defence floor.
Its final source NPC12080 can alias12077: state is an explicit enum, not an ID.
Mapped spike models are visual objects; the instance owns and restores movement
and projectile masks independently. Burn damage obeys owner/lease permissions,
the shield and pre-shield lethal cap.

The184 interface/protocol remains in use. Muspah's shield is reported in chat;
modern HUDs, sounds, varbits, music unlocks and modern hitsplat IDs are not emitted.
The captured donor labels stationary melee scaling, cloud spawn positioning and
some hazard damage as estimates. These remain estimates, not OSRS-exact balance.
Kandarin diary-specific sapphire proc bonuses and Arceuus corruption spells are
not included. Gear gaps include crystal recolour/corruption crafting and quartz
sceptre variants. ToA-specific gear multipliers have an explicit future context
hook; this batch does not supply a Tombs of Amascut encounter.
