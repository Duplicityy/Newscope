02 / Beyond the familiar

Six squares of Varlamore.
A new place to explore.

A connected patch of Varlamore terrain and scenery is staged in a separate location. Original map regions and the established home remain preserved.

  • Terrain heights preserved during relocation.
  • Scenery and compatible textures converted for revision 184.
  • Entry through ::varlamore, using the existing teleport restrictions.

This is a scenery expansion. Varlamore quests, NPCs, bosses and scripted object interactions are not included. Two decorative ship-sail placements are omitted to fit the existing renderer.

04 / Know the scope

Clear support. Clear limits.

This is a revision 242 asset backport on the revision 184 engine. It does not bring the complete 242 protocol or all modern OSRS gameplay.

When the staged release is activated

Two simple ways in.

The commands become available only when the content manifest is enabled on that world.

::newitems torva

Search the supported equipment catalogue by name or item ID. Lists supported entries without granting items.

::varlamore

Travel to the new area through the standard teleport checks. Your original home command remains available.
